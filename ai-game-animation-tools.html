Animation quality depends on more than a convincing preview. Production motion must fit the rig, preserve contact and timing, transition cleanly between states, and work with gameplay constraints. Test a representative motion in your actual character pipeline before scaling up.

Tools to investigate

The products below are starting points, not a ranked “best tools” list. Follow each official link for current capabilities and requirements, then compare the results against your own project.

What to check before choosing

  • Rig compatibility, skeleton structure, retargeting, and deformation quality
  • Foot contact, timing, transitions, and motion artifacts
  • Export formats, frame rate, and target-engine integration
  • Whether manual cleanup erases the time saved by generation

Use a small, repeatable test. Pick one representative task from your game, record the time and cleanup needed, and compare the result with your existing workflow. Do not judge a tool only by its best demo or generated preview.
