About us

Practical guidance for people who make games.

GameAITools is being built as an independent resource to help game creators understand and evaluate AI-assisted development tools.

What we want to help with

AI tools can assist with many parts of a game project, but choosing them is not always straightforward. Capabilities change, product terms vary, and a tool that is useful for a prototype may not be suitable for a production pipeline.

Our goal is to organize the landscape around real creator tasks: coding, visual assets, sound, animation, NPC behavior, and testing. We aim to explain what a tool does, where it may help, what to check before using it, and which trade-offs matter.

How we approach recommendations

We intend to distinguish verified information from vendor claims and personal evaluation. Pricing, availability, licensing, and compatibility can change, so they should be checked against official sources before a decision. We will not describe a product as hands-on tested unless that testing has actually taken place.

Where a link is an affiliate link or a placement is sponsored, the relationship should be disclosed clearly. Commercial relationships should not replace honest evaluation.

Still growing

This website is launching in stages. The initial directory contains category-level starting points; detailed product listings and guides will be added after research and validation. We welcome constructive feedback and corrections.
