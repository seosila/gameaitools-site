CATEGORY GUIDE / AUDIO

AI game audio for voices and worlds

Compare voice and interactive-audio workflows for games, from placeholder dialogue to runtime character speech and production-ready voice assets.

CATEGORY OVERVIEW · RESEARCH-BASED · UPDATED OCTOBER 2026
Home / Categories / AI Game Audio Tools for Voice, Dialogue & Sound

Audio needs differ between a fixed cinematic line, thousands of localized dialogue lines, and a character responding in real time. Choose the workflow first. Test voice direction, pronunciation, emotional range, latency, integration, and rights attached to input and output audio.

Tools to investigate

The products below are starting points, not a ranked “best tools” list. Follow each official link for current capabilities and requirements, then compare the results against your own project.

  • ElevenLabs — Explore text-to-speech and game-oriented voice workflows. Check voice rights, usage terms, and production requirements.
  • Inworld AI — Investigate real-time voice models and inference APIs for interactive media. Evaluate latency, runtime cost, quality, and integration.
  • NVIDIA ACE for Games — Developer technologies for conversational characters, including speech and character-intelligence components. Check hardware and SDK requirements.
  • Unity AI tools — Consider engine-integrated audio generation where available; verify feature access, export needs, and licensing.
  • Convai — Explore conversational character workflows that may combine dialogue with voice and interactive behavior; prototype in your target environment.

What to check before choosing

  • Commercial rights for generated voices and voice-cloning consent
  • Pronunciation, emotional direction, consistency, and localization quality
  • Runtime latency, offline needs, integration options, and platform support
  • Per-character or per-minute cost at realistic dialogue volume

Use a small, repeatable test. Pick one representative task from your game, record the time and cleanup needed, and compare the result with your existing workflow. Do not judge a tool only by its best demo or generated preview.
