Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how GameAITools.com (“GameAITools,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit the website or contact us. We aim to collect only what is reasonably needed to operate the site, respond to messages, and protect the service.

1. Who operates this website

GameAITools.com is an independent website about AI tools for game development. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form. The site operator’s legal name and business address should be added here if required by the laws that apply to the operator or the site’s activities.

2. Information you provide

If you use our contact form, we ask for your name, email address, message topic, and message. Please do not send sensitive personal information or information that is not needed to handle your enquiry.

We use this information to read, manage, and respond to your message, consider tool suggestions or corrections, and handle related follow-up. We do not use contact submissions to send a newsletter or unrelated marketing campaigns.

3. Contact form provider

Our contact form is processed by Formspree, which receives form submissions and helps deliver them to us. Your submission is sent to Formspree and may be available in its systems and in the email inbox to which notifications are delivered, subject to the services and settings in use. Formspree describes its security practices and data processing in its security information and website; please review its current privacy policy for details about its own processing.

Formspree states that its service is hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States and that it uses Standard Contractual Clauses for certain transfers when acting as a processor. Depending on your location, using the form may therefore involve processing or transfers outside your country. We do not control Formspree’s independent service operations or retention settings.

4. Hosting and technical information

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most websites, the hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information needed to deliver pages, maintain security, diagnose errors, and prevent abuse. This can include IP address, browser or device information, requested URLs, timestamps, and server or security logs, depending on provider configuration and policy.

5. Fonts, cookies, and analytics

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font resources, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request details. See Google’s Privacy Policy for information about Google’s processing.

We do not currently intentionally run our own analytics, advertising pixels, or behavioural advertising scripts on the site. The site does not require an account. We do not knowingly set first-party analytics or advertising cookies. Hosting, security, embedded services, or third-party providers may use their own technical mechanisms or process request information under their policies. This section will be updated if we add analytics, advertising, embedded media, or tracking tools.

6. Legal grounds and how we use information

Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies, the legal basis depends on the context. We may process a message to take steps you request or to respond to your enquiry, for our legitimate interests in operating and securing the site and handling correspondence, or where another legal basis applies. We will not use one legal basis as a blanket justification for every type of processing.

7. Retention and security

We keep correspondence for only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant records, resolve follow-up, or meet legal obligations, then delete or securely dispose of it where appropriate. Copies may remain in email, backups, or Formspree systems according to the settings and retention practices of those services. We cannot promise a fixed deletion period for systems whose retention we do not control.

We use reasonable measures appropriate to a small informational website, but no website, email system, or online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. When information may be shared

Information may be processed by providers that help operate the website, host its pages, process contact submissions, or deliver email. We may also disclose information where required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or to protect the rights, security, and integrity of the site and others. We do not sell contact form submissions or use them to build advertising profiles.

9. External links and future affiliate links

Our pages link to third-party tools, documentation, and websites. Once you follow an external link, that provider’s terms and privacy practices apply. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

We may introduce affiliate links or clearly labelled sponsored placements in the future. If we do, we will review this policy and describe relevant tracking or data practices before or when those features are introduced. Affiliate relationships are also addressed on our affiliate disclosure page.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the law that applies, you may have rights to request access to personal information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to certain processing, or receive a portable copy. Where processing is based on consent, you may also have the right to withdraw consent. These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

To make a request, use our contact form and describe the request. We may need to verify your identity before acting. You may also have the right to complain to your competent data protection supervisory authority.

11. Children

This site is intended as a general resource for game creators, not as a service directed to children. Please do not submit personal information about children through the contact form unless you have the authority and a lawful reason to do so.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or its services change. The “Last updated” date above indicates when this page was most recently revised. Material changes will be reflected in the published policy.

13. Contact

For questions about this policy or a privacy request, please use the GameAITools contact form.

This page is intended to describe the website’s current setup in plain language. It is not legal advice. The site operator should verify the final wording against actual provider settings, identify the responsible legal person or entity where required, and obtain qualified advice if needed for the jurisdictions served.
